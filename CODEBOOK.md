# Intent Codebook

## account_issue
Account access, login, password, verification, account lock or profile-access problems.

## billing_refund
Charges, payments, refunds, subscriptions, unexpected billing or payment disputes.

## technical_support
App/device/site errors, crashes, connectivity, features not working or technical troubleshooting.

## other
Messages that do not fit the above categories, unclear requests, general comments, or requests needing a different category.

## Escalation policy
Escalate when the message indicates a high-risk or unresolved situation such as suspected fraud/unauthorised payment, account compromise, repeated failure after normal troubleshooting, privacy/legal/safety concern, or a case requiring human-specific account action.

Otherwise auto-handle when a safe generic response can be provided without inventing account-specific facts.
